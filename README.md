<h1>The Anarchy Manifesto</h1>

States are institutions of centralized violence which create more problems than they ostensibly exist to solve. Anarchy, the absence of rulership, is achieved by hindering, dismantling, and preventing any situations where someone has power over others. In this way it is not a destination, but a direction. There is no end of the rainbow. Anarchy, often misunderstood as a war of all-against-all, functions by the fact that without rulers, individuals have to negotiate with each other. Where there is no state, no central apparatus of power to seize, the winning move is cooperation.
